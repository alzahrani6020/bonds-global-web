## Misplaced clause requiring legal review

The Privacy Policy contains a contractual limitation-of-liability section under “Shared Security Responsibility” / “مسؤولية الحماية المشتركة”, including a cap tied to amounts paid during the preceding twelve months.

A substantially corresponding limitation-of-liability clause already exists in the Terms of Use.

Engineering assessment: the contractual liability cap belongs more naturally in Terms than Privacy. However, moving, deleting, consolidating, or rewriting it changes the published legal presentation and therefore requires an approved legal source or counsel decision.

## Refund / subscription note

The Terms currently state that paid subscriptions may be cancelled and that payments are non-refundable after service activation except for cases evaluated individually under internal policy.

No separate approved Refund Policy source has been identified in the repository. Do not create one by inference.
