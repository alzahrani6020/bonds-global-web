Data Sources & Methodology

This page explains how Bonds separates traceable observed data from modeled estimates, stale data and missing evidence. The core rule is simple: an internal estimate must never be presented as an official fact.

Calculation methodology · Intelligence hub

Verified

Observed data

A value with a traceable source, fetch timestamp, reference period and known geographic scope. A generic source URL alone is not sufficient.

Modeled

Model or inference

A value derived from a model, benchmark, inference or internal transformation. It is labeled as an estimate and never replaces an observed official value.

Stale

Outside freshness policy

The value may still be historically valid but is older than the accepted freshness window for that metric.

Missing

Insufficient evidence

When traceable evidence is unavailable, the platform shows “Unavailable” instead of fabricating a number or converting a read failure into zero.

Core evidence fields

FieldMeaning
source_name / source_urlThe organization or page to which the observation can be traced.
observed_at / yearThe period described by the value, not merely the download time.
fetched_atWhen Bonds retrieved the observation.
geographic_scopeCity, region/governorate or national. National values must not be presented as city-level facts.
confidenceConfidence in measurement/mapping, not a guarantee of an investment outcome.
data_qualityExamples: official, open_data, estimated, fallback.

Freshness policy

Bonds does not use one age threshold for every metric. Each series should have a freshness_sla_days appropriate to its behavior: population can remain useful for longer periods, while prices and market indicators may require much faster updates.

The label “Live” is reserved for a real source feed with a known delay. Periodic government publications are labeled “Latest official release”, not “Live”.

Opportunity scores

Composite scores must be explainable: factors, weights, source for each factor and the share of modeled inputs. If evidence coverage is insufficient, the score should not be presented as a complete fact.

Missing-metric inference in the city data pipeline is opt-in rather than the default and remains explicitly marked as modeled.

Geographic catalog

Bonds uses canonical geographic identifiers and distinguishes cities, regions, ports, economic zones and industrial cities. Presence in the catalog does not imply that verified economic metrics are currently available.
