FAQ

Everything You Need to Know About Pricing

Is there a free trial?
Yes. Pro and Enterprise plans include a 14-day free trial. You can cancel before the trial ends and no charge will be made.
What's the difference between Pro and Enterprise?
Pro fits growing restaurants and businesses that need unlimited scenarios and PDF export. Enterprise is built for chains and large groups and includes webhook integrations and multiple sub-accounts.
Can I get a refund?
During the free trial no charge is made. After the subscription starts, you can cancel anytime and keep your features until the end of the paid period.
Is the first consultation really free?
Yes, 100%. We listen to your situation and determine if we are the right fit to help you — with no commitment or payment required.
How do I get a consulting quote?
Fill out the contact form or reach us via WhatsApp. We respond within 2–3 business days with a written quote outlining the scope, timeline, and cost.
