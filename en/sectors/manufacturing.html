️ Investor Journey (5 Stages)

1
Application Stage
  • Foreign Investor License – Invest Saudi
  • Commercial Registration – Saudi Business Center
  • Industrial License Application – Sanaie Platform
  • Contract Submission – Shariq Platform
2
Construction Stage
  • Building Permit from Municipality
  • Plan Approval from MODON
  • Building Completion Certificate
  • Utility Connections (Electricity, Water)
3
Equipment Stage
  • Equipment Installation
  • Safety System Setup
  • Pre-Operation Inspection
4
Licensing Stage
  • Operating License Application
  • Final Inspection Visit
  • Environmental Compliance
5
Operation Stage
  • Production Start
  • Periodic Inspections
  • License Renewal (every 5 years)
