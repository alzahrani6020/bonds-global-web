Industrial Investment Guide in Lebanon

Everything an investor needs: government bodies, industrial zones, licenses, and costs in the Lebanese Republic

🏛️ Key Government Bodies

🏭
Ministry of Industry
The main authority for industry. Issues industrial licenses. Develops industrial policies. Supports exports.
📈
Investment Development Authority (IDAL)
Promotes foreign direct investment. Grants incentives and tax exemptions. Coordinates with government bodies.
⚡
Electricité du Liban (EDL)
The official body for electricity generation and distribution. Suffers from severe crisis. Daily power cuts reach 22 hours.
🌊
Lebanese Water Authority
Manages water resources. Supplies factories with water. Operates water treatment plants.
🌿
Ministry of Environment
Environmental protection. Issues environmental permits. Monitors industrial emissions.
📋
Lebanese Chambers of Commerce
Commercial registration of companies. Investor support. Provides commercial and industrial information.

🏙️ Main Industrial Zones

🏭
Sidon Industrial Zone
South Lebanon. 200+ factories. Specializations: food, textiles, plastics. Near Sidon port.
🏭
Tripoli Industrial Zone
North Lebanon. Specializations: food, textiles, building materials. Near Tripoli port.
🏭
Zahle Industrial Zone
Bekaa (central Lebanon). Specializations: food, wine, agriculture and processing.
🏭
Beirut Industrial Zone
Capital. Specializations: light industry, assembly, technology. Most expensive area in Lebanon.
🏭
Jounieh Industrial Zone
Mount Lebanon. Specializations: light industry, packaging, industrial tourism.
🏭
Halba Industrial Zone
Akkar (north Lebanon). Specializations: food, agriculture, storage. Competitive land prices.
🏭
Zouk Mosbeh Industrial Zone
Keserwan (north of Beirut). Specializations: mechanical, plastics, storage.
🏭
Jezzine Industrial Zone
South Lebanon. Specializations: agriculture, packaging, cold storage.

🗺️ Investor Journey

1
Initial Licensing
  • Submit industrial license application to Ministry of Industry
  • Obtain IDAL approval (for foreign investment)
  • Register company in commercial registry
  • Prepare project feasibility study
  • Duration: 2-4 weeks
2
Land Allocation & Location
  • Select suitable industrial zone
  • Submit land allocation request to zone administration
  • Pay reservation and security deposit fees (5-10%)
  • Sign lease or purchase contract
  • Duration: 2-4 weeks
3
Construction Permits
  • Submit certified engineering plans to municipality
  • Obtain building permit
  • Obtain civil defense approval
  • Execute project through approved contractor
  • Duration: 3-6 months depending on project
4
Final Operation
  • Building completion certificate
  • Civil defense safety inspection
  • Install private generator or solar system
  • Obtain final operating license
  • Duration: 2-4 weeks

🏗️ Industrial Building Standards

📐
Building Ratios
Building ratio: 50-70%. Green spaces: 10%. Factory height: up to 12 meters.
🚒
Safety Requirements
Two emergency exits. 4-meter distance between buildings. Fire water tank. Fire alarm system.
🌿
Environmental Standards
Environmental impact assessment. Waste treatment. Emission monitoring. Compliance with Ministry of Environment standards.
👷
Labor Requirements
3.5 m² per worker. Sanitary facilities. Compliance with Lebanese labor law. Employment of Lebanese workers.
