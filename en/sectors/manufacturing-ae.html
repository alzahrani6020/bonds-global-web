Industrial Investment Guide in UAE

Everything an investor needs: authorities, free zones and industrial areas, licenses, and costs in the United Arab Emirates

🏛️ Key Government Authorities

🏭
Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology
The main body for regulating and developing the industrial sector. Oversees the National Strategy for Industry and Advanced Technology and factory classification.
📋
Department of Economic Development - Abu Dhabi
Issues industrial licenses in Abu Dhabi Emirate and regulates economic activities. Manages industrial land allocation programs.
🌆
Department of Economic Development - Dubai
Regulates the industrial sector in Dubai and issues licenses. Oversees industrial and free zones in the emirate.
⚡
Electricity and Water Authorities
DEWA (Dubai), ADDC and AADC (Abu Dhabi), SEWA (Sharjah), FEWA (Northern Emirates). Provide electricity and water services to factories.
🌊
Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone (RAKEZ)
One of the largest economic zones in the UAE. Includes diverse industrial zones and provides integrated solutions for investors.
🤝
Nafis (Emiratisation Council)
A government program to increase citizen participation in the private sector. Provides support to establishments that employ Emirati talent.

🏙️ Key Industrial and Free Zones

🏗️
Khalifa Industrial Zone (KIZAD)
Largest integrated industrial zone in the UAE. Connected to Khalifa Port (deepest artificial port in the world). Area: 417 km². Sectors: aluminum, steel, petrochemicals, logistics.
🚢
Jebel Ali Free Zone (JAFZA)
Largest free zone in the world by trade. Hosts 9,500+ companies from 135 countries. 100% tax exemption for 50 years. Near Jebel Ali Port (largest port in the region).
🏭
Industrial City of Abu Dhabi (ICAD)
5 industrial centers in Abu Dhabi (ICAD I-V). Hosts 1,200+ factories. Focus on heavy and medium industries. Owned and managed by KEZAD Group.
🏘️
Dubai Industrial City (DIP)
Largest industrial complex in Dubai. 720+ factories. Area: 28 km². Includes: factories, warehouses, offices, labor accommodation.
⚓
Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone (RAKEZ)
15,000+ companies from 100 countries. 8 specialized zones. Lowest operating cost in the UAE. Near RAK Airport and Saqr Port.
🌊
Fujairah Free Zone
Direct access to the Gulf of Oman. No Suez Canal needed. Fujairah Port (closest port for ships coming from Asia). Focus on light industries and logistics.
🏗️
Hamriyah Free Zone (HFZA)
Largest free zone in Sharjah. 7,600+ companies. Hamriyah Port (deepest port in the UAE). Licensing within 24 hours. Specializations: light and medium industries.
🏭
Al Ain Industrial Zone
Strategic location near Omani and Saudi borders. Industrial land at competitive prices. Suitable for food and packaging industries.

🗺️ Industrial Investor Journey

1
Initial License and Approvals
  • Submit industrial license application to the Department of Economic Development
  • Obtain approval from the Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology
  • Municipal approval for the site
  • Environmental impact assessment (for medium and high-impact industries)
  • Duration: 2-4 weeks
2
Land or Facility Allocation
  • Select the appropriate industrial or free zone
  • Sign lease or purchase contract with the developer
  • Pay registration and security fees (10-20% of contract value)
  • Receive site and start preparation works
  • Duration: 2-6 weeks
3
Building Permit and Construction
  • Submit approved engineering drawings to the municipality
  • Obtain building permit
  • Excavation and backfill permit if needed
  • Construction supervision by approved contractor
  • Duration: 2-6 months depending on facility size
4
Final License and Operation
  • Building completion certificate
  • Civil Defense safety inspection
  • Electricity and water connection from service provider
  • Obtain final operating license
  • Duration: 2-4 weeks

🏗️ Industrial Building Requirements

📐
Building Ratios
Building ratio: 50-70% of total area. Factory height: 12-18 meters for light industries, up to 30 meters for heavy industries. Green spaces: 10-15% mandatory.
🚒
Civil Defense Requirements
At least two emergency exits. 6 meters distance between buildings. Fire water tank (1,000-5,000 liters). Fire alarm system connected to Civil Defense. Clear evacuation corridors.
🌿
Environmental Requirements
Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) for medium and high-impact industries. Liquid waste treatment before discharge. Air emission monitoring. Compliance with Federal Environment Authority standards.
👷
Labor Requirements
Separate labor accommodation for large factories (100+ workers). 3.5 m² per worker. Kitchen and sanitary facilities. Compliance with Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation standards.
