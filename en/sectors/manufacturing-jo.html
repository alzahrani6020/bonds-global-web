Industrial Investment Guide in Jordan

Everything an investor needs: authorities, industrial zones, licenses, and costs in the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan

Key Government Authorities

Jordan Investment Commission (JIC)
The main body for promoting and facilitating investment. Issues investment licenses and grants incentives to investors. Provides one-stop shop services.
Aqaba Special Economic Zone Authority (ASEZA)
Manages the Aqaba Special Economic Zone. Grants tax and customs duty exemptions. Special legislative system for the zone.
Jordan Industrial Estates Company (JIEC)
Development and management of industrial cities. Includes 12 industrial cities. Provides industrial land at subsidized prices with full infrastructure.
National Electric Power Company (NEPCO)
The sole entity for purchasing and transmitting electricity. Sells to distribution companies. Electricity pricing is subject to the Energy and Minerals Regulatory Commission.
Water Authority of Jordan (WAJ)
Manages water resources and sewage. Provides water services to industrial zones at subsidized prices.
Ministry of Industry and Trade
Industrial activity licensing. Consumer protection. Product quality control. Industrial certificate authentication.

Industrial Cities and Key Zones

Al-Hassan Industrial City - Irbid
Largest industrial city in Jordan. 500+ factories. 70,000+ workers. Specializations: garments, pharmaceuticals, engineering, food. Near Syrian and Iraqi borders.
Sahab Industrial City
Near the capital Amman. 300+ factories. Specializations: engineering, plastics, chemicals, food. First industrial city in Jordan (1980).
Al-Qastal Industrial City
Between Amman and Zarqa. 200+ factories. Specializations: construction, finishing materials, engineering industries. Strategic location on the desert highway.
Aqaba Special Economic Zone
100% tax exemption for 25 years. Customs duties only 5%. Exemption from income and sales tax. Aqaba Port (Jordan's largest port).
Al-Muwaqqar Industrial City
Newest industrial city. Modern design and advanced infrastructure. Specializations: technology, logistics, advanced industries.
Al-Zarqa Industrial City
Specializations: chemicals, plastics, engineering industries. Near the Amman-Zarqa international highway.
Al-Karak Industrial City
Southern Jordan. Specializations: mining, food industries, dairy products. Land at competitive prices.
Al-Maan Industrial City
Southern Jordan. Specializations: phosphate and metal industries. Near phosphate mines.

Industrial Investor Journey

1
Initial License
  • Submit investment license application to the Investment Commission
  • Economic feasibility study for the project
  • Obtain approval from the Ministry of Industry and Trade
  • Register the company with the Ministry of Industry
  • Duration: 2-4 weeks
2
Land Allocation and Site
  • Select the appropriate industrial city
  • Submit land allocation application to the Industrial Estates Company
  • Pay reservation and security fees (5-10%)
  • Sign lease or purchase contract
  • Duration: 2-6 weeks
3
Building Permit and Construction
  • Submit approved engineering drawings to the city municipality
  • Obtain building permit
  • Civil Defense approval
  • Project execution by approved contractor
  • Duration: 3-8 months depending on project
4
Final Operation
  • Building completion certificate
  • Civil Defense safety inspection
  • Electricity and water connection
  • Obtain final operating license
  • Duration: 2-4 weeks

Industrial Building Requirements

Building Ratios
Building ratio: 40-60% depending on activity. Green spaces: 10%. Factory height: up to 12 meters for light industries, 18 meters for heavy industries.
Safety Requirements
At least two emergency exits. 4 meters distance between buildings. Fire water tank. Fire alarm system. Compliance with Jordanian Civil Defense standards.
Environmental Requirements
Environmental impact assessment for medium and high-impact industries. Waste treatment before discharge. Emission monitoring. Compliance with Jordanian Ministry of Environment standards.
Labor Requirements
3.5 m² per worker. Separate sanitary facilities. Kitchen and prayer room. Compliance with Jordanian labor law. Preference for Jordanian workers.
