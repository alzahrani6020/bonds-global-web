Industrial Investment Guide in Yemen

Everything an investor needs: government bodies, industrial zones, licenses, and costs in the Republic of Yemen

🏛️ Key Government Bodies

🏭
Ministry of Industry and Trade
The main authority for industry and trade. Issues industrial licenses. Develops industrial policies. Supports exports.
📈
General Investment Authority (GIA)
Promotes foreign and domestic investment. Grants incentives and tax exemptions. Coordinates with government bodies.
⚡
Public Electricity Corporation
The official body for electricity generation and distribution. Suffers from near-total shutdown. Private sector entering power generation.
🌊
Public Authority for Water and Sanitation
Manages water resources. Supplies factories with water. Operates desalination plants.
🌿
Environment Protection Authority
Environmental protection. Issues environmental permits. Monitors industrial emissions.
📋
Yemen Chamber of Commerce
Commercial registration of companies. Investor support. Provides commercial and industrial information.

🏙️ Main Industrial Zones

🏭
Sana'a Industrial Zone
Capital (under Houthi control). 300+ factories. Specializations: food, textiles, building materials. Unstable conditions.
🏭
Aden Industrial Zone
South Yemen. Specializations: maritime industry, packaging, export. Near Aden port. Relative stability.
🏭
Taiz Industrial Zone
Central Yemen. Specializations: food, building materials, storage. Unstable security situation.
🏭
Hodeidah Industrial Zone
West Yemen (Red Sea). Specializations: maritime industry, fisheries, export. Near Hodeidah port.
🏭
Mukalla Industrial Zone
East Yemen (Al Mahrah). Specializations: cross-border trade, storage, logistics.
🏭
Mocha Industrial Zone
West Yemen (Red Sea). Specializations: maritime industry, export. Near Mocha port.
🏭
Ibb Industrial Zone
Central Yemen. Specializations: agriculture and food processing, building materials.
🏭
Seiyun Industrial Zone
East Yemen (Hadramaut). Specializations: oil, gas, port-related industries.

🗺️ Investor Journey

1
Initial Licensing
  • Submit industrial license application to Ministry of Industry and Trade
  • Obtain GIA approval
  • Register company in Chamber of Commerce
  • Prepare project feasibility study
  • Duration: 4-12 weeks (depending on region)
2
Land Allocation & Location
  • Select suitable industrial city
  • Submit land allocation request to city administration
  • Pay reservation and security deposit fees (5-10%)
  • Sign lease or purchase contract
  • Duration: 4-12 weeks
3
Construction Permits
  • Submit certified engineering plans to municipality
  • Obtain building permit
  • Obtain civil defense approval
  • Execute project through approved contractor
  • Duration: 6-18 months depending on project
4
Final Operation
  • Building completion certificate
  • Civil defense safety inspection
  • Install private generator or solar system
  • Obtain final operating license
  • Duration: 2-8 weeks

🏗️ Industrial Building Standards

📐
Building Ratios
Building ratio: 50-70%. Green spaces: 10%. Factory height: up to 12 meters.
🚒
Safety Requirements
Two emergency exits. 5-meter distance between buildings. Fire water tank. Fire alarm system.
🌿
Environmental Standards
Environmental impact assessment. Waste treatment. Emission monitoring. Compliance with Environment Protection Authority standards.
👷
Labor Requirements
3.5 m² per worker. Sanitary facilities. Compliance with Yemeni labor law. Employment of local workers.
