Industrial Investment Guide in Iraq

Everything an investor needs: government bodies, industrial zones, licenses, and costs in the Republic of Iraq

🏛️ Key Government Bodies

🏭
Ministry of Industry and Minerals
The main authority for industry and minerals. Manages government industrial companies. Develops industrial policies. Issues factory licenses.
📈
National Investment Commission (NIC)
Promotes foreign and local investment. Grants incentives and exemptions. Coordinates with government bodies.
🏦
Industrial Bank of Iraq
Finances industrial projects. Concessional loans for the industrial sector. Credit guarantees for investors.
⚡
Ministry of Electricity
The sole authority for electricity generation and distribution. Provides connection services for factories. Suffers from production shortages (frequent outages).
🌿
Ministry of Environment
Environmental protection. Issues environmental permits. Monitors industrial emissions. Environmental impact assessments.
📋
Baghdad / Basra Chamber of Commerce
Company registration. Investor support. Provides commercial and industrial information.

🏙️ Major Industrial Zones

🏭
Bismaya Industrial City (Baghdad)
Iraq's largest industrial city. 3,000+ hectares. Specializations: Food, Construction, Chemicals, Engineering. Near Baghdad.
🏭
Basra Industrial City
Southern Iraq. Specializations: Oil & Petrochemicals, Marine Industries, Logistics. Near oil fields.
🏭
Sadr Industrial City
East Baghdad. Specializations: Light industries, Plastics, Metals. Suitable for small projects.
🏭
Najaf Industrial City
Southern Iraq. Specializations: Food industries, Packaging, Construction. Near pilgrimage centers.
🏭
Karbala Industrial City
Southern Iraq. Specializations: Food industries, Textiles, Construction. Near pilgrimage centers.
🏭
Erbil Industrial Zone (Kurdistan Region)
Northern Iraq. Specializations: Construction, Light industries, Assembly. Relative security stability. Tax exemptions.
🏭
Sulaymaniyah Industrial Zone
Kurdistan Region. Specializations: Food industries, Construction, Storage. Stable investment climate.
🏭
Mosul Industrial Zone
Northern Iraq. Reconstruction after liberation. Specializations: Construction, Light industries, Storage.

🗺️ Investor Journey

1
Initial Licensing
  • Submit industrial license application to Ministry of Industry and Minerals
  • Obtain approval from National Investment Commission
  • Register company at Chamber of Commerce
  • Feasibility study for the project
  • Duration: 4-8 weeks
2
Land Allocation & Site
  • Select appropriate industrial city
  • Submit land allocation request to city administration
  • Pay reservation and insurance fees (5-10%)
  • Sign lease or purchase contract
  • Duration: 4-8 weeks
3
Construction & Building Permit
  • Submit approved engineering plans to municipality
  • Obtain building permit
  • Civil defense approval
  • Project execution by approved contractor
  • Duration: 4-12 months depending on project
4
Final Operation
  • Building completion certificate
  • Civil defense safety inspection
  • Electricity connection (or install private generator)
  • Obtain final operating license
  • Duration: 2-6 weeks

🏗️ Industrial Building Requirements

📐
Building Ratios
Building ratio: 50-70%. Green spaces: 10%. Factory height: up to 15 meters.
🚒
Safety Requirements
Two emergency exits. 6-meter distance between buildings. Fire water tank. Fire alarm system.
🌿
Environmental Requirements
Environmental impact assessment. Waste treatment. Emission monitoring. Compliance with Ministry of Environment standards.
👷
Labor Requirements
3.5 m² per worker. Sanitary facilities. Compliance with Iraqi labor law. Employment of local workers.
