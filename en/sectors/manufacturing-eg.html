Industrial Investment Guide in Egypt

Everything an investor needs: authorities, industrial zones, licenses, and costs in the Arab Republic of Egypt

🏛️ Key Government Authorities

🏭
Industrial Development Authority (IDA)
The body responsible for implementing industrial policies and issuing industrial licenses. Established 2005.

📍 Address: 42 Al-Salam Axis, 90th Street, Cairo
🌐 www.ida.gov.eg
📞 +20 110 920 25XX
🏢
General Authority for Investment (GAFI)
Egypt's investment gateway. Provides incorporation and licensing services for local and foreign companies.

🌐 www.gafi.gov.eg
🏘️
New Urban Communities Authority
Development and allocation of industrial land in new cities (6th of October, El Obour, Badr, 10th of Ramadan...)
⚡
Ministry of Electricity and Renewable Energy
Supplies factories with electricity through nine distribution companies. Medium voltage for large factories.
💧
Holding Company for Water and Wastewater
Provides drinking water and sewage connections to industrial zones through its subsidiaries in the governorates.
🔬
Small and Medium Enterprises Development Agency
Financing for small projects, industrial complexes, and facilitations for machinery.

📋 Factory Licensing Procedures

1
Preliminary License
  • Submit electronic application via the authority website ida.gov.eg
  • Simplified feasibility study (project outline form)
  • Pay 10% reservation deposit of land value
  • Obtain preliminary license within 7 days (license by selection)
2
Approvals and Allocation
  • Land allocation from the Industrial Development Authority or New Urban Communities Authority
  • Environmental approval from the Environmental Affairs Agency
  • Civil Defense approval
  • Pay remaining land value or rent
3
Building License
  • Submit engineering drawings to the Industrial Development Authority
  • Building ratios: 40% minimum - 65% maximum
  • Height approval (7 days for areas outside aerial easement)
  • Obtain building permit from the local authority
4
Operation
  • Install meters (electricity - water - sewage)
  • Field inspection visit
  • Obtain final operating license
  • Start production (timeline: 36 months maximum from allocation)

🏗️ Industrial Building Requirements

📐
Building Ratios
Minimum 40% and maximum 65%. Some activities have special ratios (Resolution 461 of 2018).
🏗️
Heights
Height approval within 7 days for areas outside aerial easement. 15 days for special cases.
🚧
Timeline
Building permit within 4-6 months. Foundation completion within 12 months. Production start within 36 months.
🛡️
Safety and Environment
Civil Defense approval + environmental approval. GSP requirements for pharmaceutical industries.
