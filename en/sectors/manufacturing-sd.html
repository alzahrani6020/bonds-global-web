Industrial Investment Guide in Sudan

Everything an investor needs: government bodies, industrial zones, licenses, and costs in the Republic of Sudan

🏛️ Key Government Bodies

🏭
Ministry of Industry and Trade
The main authority for industry and trade. Issues industrial licenses. Develops industrial policies. Supports exports.
📈
Sudanese Investment Authority
Promotes foreign and local investment. Grants incentives and exemptions. Coordinates with government bodies.
⚡
Sudan Electricity Holding Company (SEDC)
The main authority for electricity generation and distribution. Provides connection services. Suffers from production shortages.
💧
National Water Corporation
Manages water resources. Supplies water to factories. Irrigation and dam projects.
🌿
Environmental Protection Authority
Environmental protection. Issues environmental permits. Monitors industrial emissions.
🏗️
Sudan Chamber of Commerce and Industry
Company registration. Investor support. Provides commercial and industrial information.

🏙️ Major Industrial Zones

🏭
Omdurman Industrial Zone
Sudan's largest industrial zone. 500+ factories. Specializations: Food, Textiles, Construction, Engineering. Near the capital.
🏭
Khartoum Bahri Industrial Zone
North Khartoum. Specializations: Food industries, Plastics, Metals. Near Bahri Nile port.
🏭
Port Sudan Industrial Zone
Eastern Sudan (Red Sea). Specializations: Marine industries, Packaging, Export. Near Port Sudan main port.
🏭
Jili Industrial Zone
North Khartoum. Specializations: Textiles, Leather industries, Plastics. Integrated industrial area.
🏭
Hasaheisa Industrial Zone
Central Sudan (Gezira). Specializations: Agricultural industries, Sugar, Textiles. Near irrigated agriculture projects.
🏭
Dongola Industrial Zone
Northern Sudan. Specializations: Dates, Agricultural industries, Mining. Near gold fields.
🏭
Wad Madani Industrial Zone
Central Sudan. Specializations: Food industries, Textiles, Construction. Near agriculture projects.
🏭
Kassala Industrial Zone
Eastern Sudan. Specializations: Food industries, Livestock. Near Eritrean border.

🗺️ Investor Journey

1
Initial Licensing
  • Submit industrial license application to Ministry of Industry and Trade
  • Obtain approval from Sudanese Investment Authority
  • Register company at Chamber of Commerce and Industry
  • Feasibility study for the project
  • Duration: 4-8 weeks
2
Land Allocation & Site
  • Select appropriate industrial city
  • Submit land allocation request to city administration
  • Pay reservation and insurance fees (5-10%)
  • Sign lease or purchase contract
  • Duration: 4-8 weeks
3
Construction & Building Permit
  • Submit approved engineering plans to municipality
  • Obtain building permit
  • Civil defense approval
  • Project execution by approved contractor
  • Duration: 4-12 months depending on project
4
Final Operation
  • Building completion certificate
  • Civil defense safety inspection
  • Electricity connection (or install private generator)
  • Obtain final operating license
  • Duration: 2-6 weeks

🏗️ Industrial Building Requirements

📐
Building Ratios
Building ratio: 50-70%. Green spaces: 10%. Factory height: up to 12 meters.
🚒
Safety Requirements
Two emergency exits. 5-meter distance between buildings. Fire water tank. Fire alarm system.
🌿
Environmental Requirements
Environmental impact assessment. Waste treatment. Emission monitoring. Compliance with Environmental Protection Authority standards.
👷
Labor Requirements
3.5 m² per worker. Sanitary facilities. Compliance with Sudanese labor law. Employment of local workers.
