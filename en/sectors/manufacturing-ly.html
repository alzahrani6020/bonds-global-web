Industrial Investment Guide in Libya

Everything an investor needs: government bodies, industrial zones, licenses, and costs in the State of Libya

🏛️ Key Government Bodies

🏭
Ministry of Economy and Trade
The main authority for industry. Issues industrial licenses. Develops industrial policies. Supports exports.
📈
Libyan Investment Authority (LIA)
Promotes foreign and domestic investment. Grants incentives and tax exemptions. Coordinates with government bodies.
⚡
General Electricity Company of Libya (GECOL)
The official body for electricity generation and distribution. Suffers from severe shortages. Daily power cuts reach 22 hours.
🌊
General Water Authority
Manages water resources. Supplies factories with water. Operates desalination plants.
🌿
Environment General Authority
Environmental protection. Issues environmental permits. Monitors industrial emissions.
📋
Libyan Chambers of Commerce
Commercial registration of companies. Investor support. Provides commercial and industrial information.

🏙️ Main Industrial Zones

🏭
Misurata Industrial Zone
Central Libya. 500+ factories. Specializations: food, textiles, building materials. Near Misurata port.
🏭
Benghazi Industrial Zone
Eastern Libya. Specializations: food, textiles, plastics. Near Benghazi port.
🏭
Tripoli Industrial Zone
Capital. Specializations: light industry, assembly, technology. Most expensive area in Libya.
🏭
Sebha Industrial Zone
Southern Libya. Specializations: agriculture and food processing, building materials.
🏭
Zawia Industrial Zone
Western Libya (near Tunisia). Specializations: food, oil refining, packaging.
🏭
Khoms Industrial Zone
Central coast. Specializations: food, textiles, storage. Near Khoms port.
🏭
Ajelat Industrial Zone
Western Libya. Specializations: agriculture, packaging, cold storage.
🏭
Tobruk Industrial Zone
Eastern Libya. Specializations: fisheries, packaging, storage. Near Tobruk port.

🗺️ Investor Journey

1
Initial Licensing
  • Submit industrial license application to Ministry of Economy
  • Obtain approval from Investment Authority
  • Register company in commercial registry
  • Prepare project feasibility study
  • Duration: 4-12 weeks
2
Land Allocation & Location
  • Select suitable industrial zone
  • Submit land allocation request to zone administration
  • Pay reservation and security deposit fees (5-10%)
  • Sign lease or purchase contract
  • Duration: 4-12 weeks
3
Construction Permits
  • Submit certified engineering plans to municipality
  • Obtain building permit
  • Obtain civil defense approval
  • Execute project through approved contractor
  • Duration: 6-18 months depending on project
4
Final Operation
  • Building completion certificate
  • Civil defense safety inspection
  • Install private generator or solar system
  • Obtain final operating license
  • Duration: 2-8 weeks

🏗️ Industrial Building Standards

📐
Building Ratios
Building ratio: 50-70%. Green spaces: 10%. Factory height: up to 12 meters.
🚒
Safety Requirements
Two emergency exits. 5-meter distance between buildings. Fire water tank. Fire alarm system.
🌿
Environmental Standards
Environmental impact assessment. Waste treatment. Emission monitoring. Compliance with Environment Authority standards.
👷
Labor Requirements
3.5 m² per worker. Sanitary facilities. Compliance with Libyan labor law. Employment of local workers.
