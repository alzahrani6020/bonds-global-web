FAQ

What You Need to Know About Funding Extraction

Is funding extraction free?

The initial consultation and file review are free. Service fees vary depending on the financing size and file complexity, and are agreed upon transparently before starting.

What documents are required?

Typically: commercial registration, financial statements for the last 3 years, bank statement, project estimates, and owner ID. Requirements may vary by financing type and entity.

How long does the process take?

We respond to your request within 24 business hours. The total duration depends on the financing entity and document completeness, typically ranging from 2 to 6 weeks.

Do you work with individuals or only companies?

We work with companies, institutions, factories, hotels, and clinics. We also assist individuals with real estate mortgages and personal project financing.

Can you help if my application was previously rejected?

Yes. We analyze the reasons for previous rejection and rebuild the file to meet the requirements of other financing entities or the same entity after improvement.
