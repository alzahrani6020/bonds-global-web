How Does the Calculator Work?

We use internationally recognized pricing formulas in managerial accounting.

📐 Mathematical Formulas

Total Cost = Direct + Overhead + Packaging + Delivery + CAC
Selling Price before Fees = Total Cost ÷ (1 − Desired Margin)
Platform Selling Price = Price before Fees ÷ (1 − Platform Fee)
Net Profit = Net Revenue − Total Cost − Tax − Zakat

⚠️ Warnings & Clarifications

  • This is an estimation tool and not a substitute for a licensed accountant.
  • Results depend on the accuracy of your inputs.
  • Tax and zakat are calculated on net profit.
  • Break-even price = total cost per unit + monthly fixed cost ÷ sales volume.
  • Platforms deduct a fee that is added on top of the price before tax.
  • The formula is based on the internationally recognized profit margin on price principle.
