المعادلات الرياضية

Installment = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n − 1]
P = صافي Loan Amount | r = نسبة Interest الدورية | n = عدد الأقساط
معدل Interest الفعلي = (1 + r)^الدورات_في_السنة − 1

  • This calculator gives you a smart initial financial view based on your data; it helps you evaluate your loan before deciding. For an accurate financial plan, consult one of our licensed advisors.
  • Declining balance interest is calculated on the remaining principal; fixed (flat) interest is calculated on the original loan amount for the entire term.
  • Results تعتمد على دقة البيانات المدخلة.
