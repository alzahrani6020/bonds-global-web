How the Engine Works

Valuation is built on 10 axes and 3 core methodologies, combining more than 30 financial, market and operational factors.

Book Value Approach

Original cost, improvements, depreciation and impairment analysis.

Market Approach

Comparable transactions and market multiples adjusted for supply and demand.

Income Approach

Future cash-flow projections, discount rates and terminal growth.

Risk Adjustment

Regulatory, environmental, market and operational risks are embedded in final value.
