FAQ

Everything You Need to Know

How do I start and what happens after I submit a request?
Reach out via the contact form or WhatsApp. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days to confirm the service type or request more information.
How long does an advisory engagement take?
It depends on scope: an exploratory session may be one or two meetings; financial analysis or budgeting can take from a few weeks to two months.
How are prices determined?
Usually as a package or phased engagement based on scope, or as a written proposal after understanding your request. We prefer full transparency in every proposal.
Do you guarantee ROI or funding approval?
No. We provide analysis and advisory based on your information; decisions and outcomes remain your responsibility.
