Supplier Account Management Guide

A comprehensive guide for managing supplier accounts on the MODON Supplier Portal

November 2023 | Saudi Authority for Industrial Cities and Technology Zones | Official MODON Website ↗

Table of Contents

  1. Home Page
  2. General Company Information

  7. Products & Services
  8. Banking Details
1

Home Page

Path: Login to Supplier Portal → Home → Management
  • Supplier logs in to the Supplier Portal
  • Quick access to company-level information
  • Manage account and services from a centralized dashboard
2

General Company Information

Path: Supplier Portal → Home → Management → General
  • Quick view of company-level information
  • Add attachments to company profile
  • Update basic data easily
3

Company Profile

Path: Supplier Portal → Home → Management → Company Profile
  • Define/update detailed supplier company information
  • Commercial registration number, location, and project name
  • Use left navigation bar to access different pages
  • Organization page defines high-level company details
4

Address Directory

Path: Supplier Portal → Home → Management → Address Book
  • Enter company location information (purchase, payment, quotes)
  • Create and modify multiple addresses
  • Delete address via trash icon
  • Update address via pencil icon
  • Add new address via "Create" button
5

Contact Directory

Path: Supplier Portal → Home → Management → Contact Directory
  • List company employees as contacts with MODON
  • Delete contact via trash icon
  • Update contact information via pencil icon
  • Manage contact address information via address icon
6

Business Classifications

Path: Supplier Portal → Home → Management → Business Classifications
  • Add/update classifications appropriate to your business
  • Go to General Information → Business Classifications
  • Select all classifications applicable to the company
  • Help MODON create supplier invitation lists
7

Products & Services

Path: Supplier Portal → Home → Management → Products & Services
  • Register products and services offered by the company
  • Link products to appropriate classifications
  • Update product details and specifications
8

Banking Details

Path: Supplier Portal → Home → Management → Banking
  • Add and manage bank account information
  • IBAN, bank name, and account details
  • Required for payment processing
