Privacy Policy

How We Protect Your Data

At Bonds, we are committed to the highest standards of data protection and transparency. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Last updated: June 11, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use the Bonds platform, we may collect the following:

  • Account data: business name, email, mobile number, password.
  • Business data: country, city, business type, employee and branch count, bio, and stated needs.
  • Financial inputs: numbers you enter into calculators or feasibility models (costs, revenue, prices) used only to produce results for your account.
  • Usage data: browser type, IP address, visited pages, and interactions with tools — to improve performance and user experience.
  • Payment data: processed through external gateways (Stripe / Moyasar); we do not store card numbers on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide your account and manage your subscription.
  • Run calculators and generate reports and analytics.
  • Communicate with you about services, updates, and support.
  • Improve platform performance and develop new features.
  • Comply with legal and tax obligations.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data based on:

  • Your explicit consent when registering and choosing to use services.
  • Contractual necessity to deliver the services you requested.
  • Legitimate interest in improving security and service quality.
  • Legal obligations applicable in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

We never sell your data. We only share data with:

  • Technical service providers (e.g., Supabase for hosting and authentication, Stripe/Moyasar for payments, Vercel for hosting).
  • Regulatory or judicial authorities when legally required.
  • Internal consulting team when you request direct consulting services.

5. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar analytics technologies to understand how the platform is used and remember your preferences. You can adjust browser settings to reject cookies, though some features may be affected.

We also use internal analytics to track currently online users, the pages they visit, and the time spent on each page, in order to improve performance and user experience. IP addresses are stored anonymously (hashed) and browsing data is not linked to individuals unless they are logged in.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. These include encryption, secure authentication, and periodic backups. However, no absolute security can be guaranteed over the internet.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and correct your data.
  • Delete your account and personal data.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing activities.
  • Request a copy of your data in a readable format.

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@bonds-global.com.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active or as necessary to provide services. Upon account deletion, we delete or anonymize personal data within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required by law.

Page view and presence data are retained for a maximum of 90 days, after which they are automatically removed or anonymized.

9. Children's Privacy

The platform is intended for adults and business owners. We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted with the last review date, and continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of changes.
