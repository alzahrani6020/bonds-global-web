Partner Portal User Guide

Registration steps and company management on the Saudi Partner Portal

January 2026 | Official User Guide | Official MODON Website ↗
1

National Single Sign-On (Nafath) Login

  • The business owner accesses the National Single Sign-On portal
  • Clicks "Login" or "Register"
  • Enters email and password for Nafath, then logs in
  • Completes registration by entering email, password, and confirmation, then submits
  • Receives a verification link via registered email
  • Enters the mobile verification code to confirm phone number
  • Confirmation message appears and email verification link is sent
  • Clicks the email link to confirm the account
2

Partner Portal Login & Company Registration

  • Login via email/password or National Single Sign-On
  • Receives SMS with verification code, enters it, and clicks "Confirm"
    Note: If code not received, click "Resend". To change phone number, click "Change Phone Number".
  • Selects "Manage My Accounts"
  • Clicks "Add New Company Data"
  • Selects the Unified National Number from dropdown to retrieve data, then clicks "Save"
  • Updates "Company Information" section and clicks "Save Data"
  • Selects "Commercial Registration Information" and clicks "Update CR and VAT Data"
3

Add Industrial License Information (if applicable)

  • Select "Industrial License Information" and click "Add Industrial License"
  • Enters "Industrial License Number" then clicks "Search", then clicks "Add"
  • License is added to the company and can be reviewed or deleted
4

Add or Remove Delegates

  • Open "Delegates List"
  • Click "Add New Delegate"
  • Enter delegate data then click "Add"
  • Delegate is added and can be deleted or replaced
5

Add or Remove Contact Person

  • Open "Delegates and Contacts List"
  • Click "Add Me as Contact" or "Add Contact Person"
  • Enter contact person data then click "Add"
  • Contact person is added and can be deleted or replaced
    Note: If the owner does not wish to add or remove a contact, the current contact will be retained.
